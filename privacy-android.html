SpeakTiles — Android app (Google Play)
Developer: Aiwa Int · Contact: aiwainit2023@gmail.com

This is the privacy policy of the SpeakTiles app for Android. By using it you agree to the points below.

1. Data collected

  • Anonymous usage analytics (GameAnalytics). A random device identifier, device type, app version, session length and which lessons you start and finish (with accuracy), used only to improve the app. No names or contact details.
  • Saved progress. Your lesson ratings, settings and unfinished sessions are stored locally on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The app shows an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a lesson session. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's Advertising ID and other technical data (such as device type, IP address for approximate location, and ad interactions) to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. In some regions (for example the EEA and the UK) you are first asked for your consent. We use no other ad network.
  • Speech. If you turn on sentence read-aloud, the text is spoken by your device's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio.

2. What we do not do

  • We never ask for your name or email, or ask you to create an account.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13; it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Your choices

  • You can delete the locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by reinstalling the app.
  • To have the analytics data linked to your device deleted, email aiwainit2023@gmail.com with the subject "SpeakTiles data deletion"; we delete it within 30 days.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device's Google settings. Where it is offered, the Ad privacy button in the app's Settings changes your consent for ads.
